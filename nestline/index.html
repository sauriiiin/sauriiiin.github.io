Nestline

A simple, on-device investment growth calculator for iPhone

What is Nestline?

Nestline is a minimalist tool designed to help you understand how an investment can grow over time. It focuses on clarity, simplicity, and privacy.

All calculations run locally on your device. No accounts, no tracking, and no data leaves your phone.

How it works

  • Enter an initial investment amount
  • Select how often growth is applied
  • Add one or more periods with duration, contribution, and growth rate
  • View portfolio value, contributions, and growth over time

Key characteristics

  • Runs entirely on-device
  • No network access
  • No analytics or tracking
  • Remembers inputs between launches
  • Supports light and dark mode

Privacy Policy

Nestline does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

All information entered into the app remains entirely on your device. Nestline does not use analytics, tracking technologies, or network services.

Because no data is collected, no data is shared with third parties.
