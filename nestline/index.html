Nestline

A simple, on-device investment growth calculator for iPhone

What is Nestline?

Nestline is a minimalist tool that helps you understand how an investment can evolve over time. It focuses on clarity, simplicity, and privacy.

All calculations run locally on your device. No accounts, no tracking, and no data leaves your phone.

How it works

  • Enter an initial investment amount
  • Select how often growth is applied (annual, monthly, etc.)
  • Add one or more periods with:
    • Duration (years)
    • Monthly contribution or withdrawal
    • Annual growth rate
  • View portfolio value, contributions, and growth over time

Why use it?

  • Model different savings and investing phases
  • Simulate deposits and withdrawals
  • Compare money invested vs. portfolio value
  • Keep scenarios realistic and easy to understand

Key characteristics

  • Runs entirely on-device
  • No network access
  • No analytics or tracking
  • Remembers inputs between launches
  • System, light, and dark appearance support

Nestline is for educational purposes only and does not provide financial advice.
